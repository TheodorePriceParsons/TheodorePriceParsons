### Hi, I'm Theo 👋

Engineering Physics student at UBC, working on mechatronics, controls and embedded systems. I like taking projects from schematic to firmware to a working board.

**Start here:**

- **[Custom-FOC-ESC](https://github.com/TheodorePriceParsons/Custom-FOC-ESC)**: field-oriented control motor driver designed from scratch. 4-layer PCB with a DRV8305 gate driver, MT6701 magnetic encoder and STM32G474 firmware.
- **[SENSOR_ESP](https://github.com/TheodorePriceParsons/SENSOR_ESP)**: sensor board for our ENPH 253 competition robot. ESP32-S3 firmware for IR beacon detection (Goertzel lock-in on DMA-sampled ADC) and PCNT metal detection, plus a custom KiCad carrier board.
- **[continuous-servo-pcb](https://github.com/TheodorePriceParsons/continuous-servo-pcb)**: my first PCB, a continuous servo controller taken from breadboard testing to a finished board.
